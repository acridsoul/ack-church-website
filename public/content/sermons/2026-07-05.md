## English Service Notes

**Preacher:** Lay Reader Damaris
**Bible Verses:** Isaiah 6:1-7, John 15:5-16
**Theme:** Uzziah must die in us so that we can see the lord
### Sermon Notes

1. You are the salt of the world
2. Do not relax and think you have reached your peak
3. Tamani kumjua mungu
4. Do not blindly trust human beings
5. The lord will make the uzziah in you to die
6. Let us connect with God in a personal way
7. You can't say you are saved and you don't recognize the authority of the lord
8. May God help me to see him as my savior and King
9. How do I react when someone has wronged me?
10. May God touch our tongues
11. Availability is a sign of humbleness
12. God has chosen and appointed you to go and bear fruits that last
13. From today you are on a mission of evangelism
