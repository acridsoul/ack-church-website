## English Service Notes

**Preacher:** Tr. Elizabeth

**Bible Verses:** Daniel 6:1-17, Matthew 4:1-11

**Theme:** Self-discipline

### Sermon Notes

1. We should be very careful in everything we do
2. We should have self-discipline
3. Self-discipline helps to make wise decisions
4. When we don't have self-discipline, we end up sinning
5. When we don't have self-discipline, we miss the blessings of God
