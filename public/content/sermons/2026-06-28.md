## English Service Notes

**Theme:** Kanisa Mashinani (Church at the Grassroots)

### Sermon Notes

There was no Sunday service at the main church on this day. The congregation met in their respective prayer cells, known as **Kanisa Mashinani** (Church at the Grassroots).

## Kikuyu Service Notes

**Theme:** Kanisa Mashinani

### Maandĩko ma Ũhoro

Gũtiakorũo na kĩrĩkanĩro kĩa Kiumia gĩthĩ kanisa-inĩ nene. Athingi nĩmacemanirie o mũng'etha-inĩ wao ũrĩa wĩtagwo **Kanisa Mashinani**.
