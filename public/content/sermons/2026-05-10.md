## English Service Notes

**Preacher:** Lay Reader Damaris

**First reading:** Isaiah 53:1-12

**Readings:** 1 Samuel 3:1-10, Luke 9:1-17

**Theme:** Mentorship in our Career

### Sermon Notes

1. In everything that you do, do it for the glory of the Lord
2. Our first mentor is God
3. Sin makes God silent
4. Let us be good mentors
5. Who have you mentored?
6. Obedience in small matters brings a lot of breakthrough
7. There is a God who speaks
8. Let us be spiritually alive
9. Parents should be ready to mentor other people
10. May the Lord give us an instructed tongue
