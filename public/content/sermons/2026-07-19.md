## English Service Notes

**Preacher:** Vicar Henry Kinyua
**Bible Verses:** Nehemiah 2:1-10, Mark 16:10-20
**Theme:** Mission in the Workplace
### Sermon Notes

1. God is calling us to move towards the mission work
2. We fail to do the mission work because of Fear
3. It is only after an encounter with Jesus that you will be able to do the mission work
4. Our Mission work is to force Demons out of People
5. We should not pretend that things are okay when they are not (Neh 1:3)
6. We should LEAVE our Comfort Zones
7. Willingness to do mission work will receive Heavenly backups from God
8. Mission Work has a lot of enemies but we will conquer them in Jesus Name!
