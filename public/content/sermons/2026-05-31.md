## English Service Notes

**Preacher:** Lay Reader Consolata

**Bible Verses:** Genesis 41:33-43, Luke 5:1-7

**Theme:** The Gift Of Wisdom

### Sermon Notes

1. Let us use the gifts God has given us to progress in life
2. The Holy spirit will teach us everything
3. Cast our nets deeper
4. We are encouraged to work hard
5. Let us desire to be filled by the Holy Spirit
6. Let us seek the gift of Wisdom
7. If any of you lacks wisdom, he should ask God who gives generously 
to all without finding fault, and it will be given to him.

Let us live in harmony
Only God can fulfill the promises he has given us
