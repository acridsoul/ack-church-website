## English Service Notes

1. God has called us to be stewards
2. We should be good stewards
3. We are not owners; we are managers of God's resources
4. Faithful stewardship includes honoring God with our resources and giving generously.
5. God has given every believer abilities, spiritual gifts, and opportunities to serve.

Stewardship is not about how much we have; it is about how faithfully we manage what God has given us.
